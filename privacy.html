Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Ipsewa publishes videos to the TikTok account of the person who authorizes it.

What we access

When you log in with TikTok we receive an access token and basic profile info (display name) so we can upload videos you choose to your account.

What we store

We only keep the access token needed to post for you. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Videos

Videos you upload are sent to TikTok on your request and are not kept by us after publishing.

Deleting your data

You can revoke access in TikTok settings, or email us to delete any stored token.
